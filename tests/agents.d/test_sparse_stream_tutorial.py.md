# test_sparse_stream_tutorial.py

- The 32-page cap leaves seven pages beyond the required 5×5 camera window:
  enough for a valid residency pass while still forcing LRU eviction as the
  cohort moves.
- The 2,200-tick native horizon crosses the far-right turnaround and the old
  roughly 1,945-tick remote-goal stall point. Keep it beyond that boundary if
  agent cadence changes.
- The 72-step browser probe is the compact deterministic interval that moves
  the camera far enough to expose newly generated, retained, and evicted
  chunks. Recalibrate it against the native model if movement cadence changes.
- The short 844×390 landscape viewport is deliberate: it makes the standalone
  content taller than the viewport and proves vertical reachability without
  weakening the no-horizontal-overflow contract.
