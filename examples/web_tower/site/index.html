What this shows

The world is one tess world six floors tall, not six stacked two-dimensional maps. Floors are separated by solid slabs, and the stairwells are the only tiles that connect one floor to the next, so an agent crossing floors is following a single route through three dimensions rather than being teleported between levels.

The ground floor and the top floor are open plates holding the starting positions and the goals. The four floors between them carry rooms and wide corridors. Close a stairwell and watch the routes redistribute to the others.

This is an early version: the geometry is deliberately simple and the camera is fixed to four orientations. See the colony demo for wall painting on a single floor, and the congestion page for pricing policies.
